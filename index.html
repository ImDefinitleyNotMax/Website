About Me

Hi, I'm Max Crothers, a passionate Director of Photography and Colourist with a keen eye for visual storytelling. With years of experience in the film and television industry, I specialize in creating compelling visuals that enhance the narrative and evoke emotion. My work spans various genres, from commercials to feature films, and I thrive on collaborating with creative teams to bring their vision to life. When I'm not behind the camera or at the color grading suite, I enjoy exploring new techniques and staying updated with the latest trends in cinematography.
