About Me

I’m Max Crothers, a Director of Photography and colourist based in Northern Ireland, working to build a career in the camera department of the local film industry. I’ve been credited as DoP on 8+ productions, including short films, documentaries, advertisements and a talk filmed and edited for the Royal Television Society. I studied Creative Media Practice at Belfast Metropolitan College, finishing with a DDD* in my BTEC Level 3 Extended Diploma, and I also completed the Belfast Met Digital Skills Academy.
